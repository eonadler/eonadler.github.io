Outreach

I run AstroReach, a hands-on astronomy education program for high school students.

AstroReach SD is currently running at The Preuss School. I taught the first course, Dark Matter & Python, in Winter 2025, supported by the UCSD School of Physical Sciences (UCSD Today story).

AstroReach LA was held at USC Hybrid High School in Spring 2022. I taught Dark Matter & Data Visualization, supported by a Carnegie Outreach Grant and run in partnership with Create Now, an arts education organization that empowers underserved youth throughout Los Angeles. All course materials are online.

Earlier, I ran outreach programs at Carnegie Observatories for local high schools (2021–2024) and was a mentor for Stanford Future Advancers of Science and Technology (2017–2019).
