Dark matter physics on small scales

On large scales, dark matter behaves like a cold, collisionless fluid (CDM). However, dark matter physics beyond gravity can drastically alter the abundance and internal structure of low-mass halos. Our simulations predict these signatures for observations of dwarf galaxies, strong gravitational lenses, and stellar streams. Each image below shows the same simulated Milky Way; drag the slider to transition from CDM to other dark matter models.
