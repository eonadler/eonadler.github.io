Galaxy formation

How did the smallest galaxies form?

  • What is the halo mass threshold for galaxy formation?
  • When and how does gas cool to form stars in ultra-faint dwarf galaxies?
  • How does our local reionization history impact dwarf galaxy evolution?

Dark matter

What is the nature of dark matter?

  • Do completely dark (galaxy-free) halos exist?
  • What are the astrophysical signatures of dark matter physics beyond gravity?
  • Can we combine small-scale structure observables to detect a deviation from CDM?

Near-field cosmology

How can observations of the local universe inform cosmology?

  • Are there features in the small-scale linear power spectrum?
  • How can we efficiently and accurately model structure in the local universe?
  • What can near-field cosmology reveal about early universe dynamics and inflation?

Collaborations and funding

Targeted Simons Research Group on Dark Matter Dynamics

Simons Foundation · PI (UC San Diego node) · from January 2027

Connecting particle physics models of dark matter with new forces to predictions for galaxies, using simulations and semi-analytic models. UC San Diego is one of the group's nodes.

Collaborative Research: Reconstructing Primordial Density Fluctuations using Near-Field Cosmology

National Science Foundation · PI (NSF AAG) · 2024–

Reconstructing the primordial power spectrum with near-field cosmology by combining simulations, including COZMIC, and semi-analytic models with dwarf galaxy data. A collaboration between UC San Diego, USC, and Carnegie Observatories.

Dark matter constraints and predictions

Our simulations and models make predictions for small-scale structure; observations of dwarf galaxies and strong gravitational lenses turn them into constraints.

Observational Constraints

Dwarf galaxies

  • The first Milky Way satellite limits on ultralight dark matter produced after inflationNadler et al. 2026
  • Limits on dark matter–Standard Model interactions and warm dark matterNadler et al. 2021a
  • The faint-end galaxy–halo connection and the smallest halos that host galaxiesNadler et al. 2020

In progress: hydrodynamic simulations of ultra-faint galaxies orbiting Milky Way analogs, to measure the galaxy formation threshold with Rubin.

Strong gravitational lenses

  • A self-interacting dark matter explanation for the dense perturber in SDSS J0946+1006Nadler, Yang & Yu 2023
  • Flux ratios combined with dwarf galaxies: the most stringent warm dark matter limit at the timeNadler et al. 2021b

In progress: Symphony-Lens, simulations that place quadruply imaged quasar lenses in realistic cosmic environments.
