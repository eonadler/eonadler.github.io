Transfer functions

  • Thermal WDM: Viel+05, or Bode+01 and Lovell+14 (ν = 1) where the paper used them.
  • Sterile neutrinos: the thermal relic with the same half-mode wavenumber (approximate). Runs without a compiled T(k) appear in panel a only.
  • FDM: Hu, Barkana & Gruzinov 2000.
  • DM–baryon scattering and ETHOS: Bohr+20 shapes with example parameters, no second peak (approximate).
  • Mixed cold + warm or fuzzy: (1 − f) + f TX (approximate).

Hollow bold segments mark approximate transfer functions. Suites that sample a range of models are drawn as a shaded band, or as the two edge models for oscillating DAO shapes.

Resolved wavenumbers

k(M) = π / RL(M),   M = (4π/3) ρ̄m RL³

Mlim = 10.1 ρ̄m d kpeak−2 is the Wang & White (2007) spurious-halo mass, with d the mean interparticle spacing and kpeak the peak of Δ²(k). The halo-mass axis above each small multiple uses the same k(M).

What is counted

Markers group runs of one suite that share family, run type, simulated mass and particle mass to 0.1 dex. Marker area grows as √N, capped at 300 runs so the largest suites do not bury the rest.
